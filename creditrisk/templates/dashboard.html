Credit card default scorecard

Who will miss next month's card payment, and where should a lender draw the line? A transparent points scorecard against a gradient-boosting model on 30,000 cardholders, a fairness audit, and the break-even cut-off as the State Bank of Pakistan's policy rate moves. The cardholders are from Taiwan (2005); the cost of money is Pakistan's.

Cardholder data: UCI Machine Learning Repository, "Default of Credit Card Clients" (Yeh and Lien, 2009), CC BY 4.0. Pakistan: State Bank of Pakistan policy rate (typed in from announcements), World Bank bank and credit series (CC BY 4.0). Card APR: MCB credit card summary box, July to December 2026. A learning project, not lending advice. Code, memo and method: github.com/Maira-W/credit-risk-scorecard. MIT licence.
